Personal instance

MetaMCP

MetaMCP is an application for configuring Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, grouping them into namespaces, and making the tools you choose available through MCP endpoints.

This privately operated instance is used by its owner. Sign-in is required to use its management features. Connections to Google services are optional and are made only when the account owner chooses to connect a Google account.

Google Workspace integration

When enabled, the integration can provide access to Google services such as Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets. It uses user-authorized OAuth access. Available tools and permissions depend on the scopes explicitly enabled by the account owner. Read access is distinct from write or delete access; Google re-consent is required for supported write permissions.

See the Privacy Policy for details about Google data and the Terms of Service for conditions of use.
